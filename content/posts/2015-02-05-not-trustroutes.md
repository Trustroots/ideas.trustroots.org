This is a tiny post to help people who are trying to find us under other words.

It's not

*Trustroutes.*

But if you're googling this name we want you to find us nevertheless. Same for

*trust routes*

,

 *trustroute*

and

 *trust route*

. It's quite a nice name as well, especially when it comes to hitchhiking. But we somehow chose Trustroots, probably because we don't want the network to be exclusively for hitchhikers in the long run.

More about the name Trustroots in

[our FAQ](https://www.trustroots.org/#!/faq)

.
