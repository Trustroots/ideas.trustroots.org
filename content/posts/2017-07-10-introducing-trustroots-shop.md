We have just launched [**a shop for Trustroots**](https://shop.trustroots.org/) t-shirts and apparel — something our members have been asking for a while now.

We want a world that encourages trust, adventure and intercultural connections. This is another way to show you are part of Trustroots family and promote that the willingness to help each other is universal, where ever we go.

### About designs

Currently there are just some basic designs available. We will be adding more little by little. If you have a great design or sketch, feel free to [send us](https://www.trustroots.org/contact) your designs so we could increase the variety!

At the shop you can use "[create](https://shop.trustroots.org/#!create)" feature to play with designs and create your unique t-shirt. You could add a gathering name or a message to keep great memories of Trustroots events. Check it out! Note that it might cost a little extra.

[![](/images/2017/07/trustroots-shop.png)](https://shop.trustroots.org)

### About prices

The Trustroots Foundation receives no income from any transaction, so you just pay exactly the cost of each item. Just growing Community.

The shop is hosted on [Spreadshirt](https://www.spreadshirt.com/about-us-C68) a worldwide respected custom merchandise printer.

There is **15% discount** on all items **for the next 10 days**.

### Maker's gonna make their own!

You can totally do this on your own as well! Grab our [logo design](https://github.com/Trustroots/media/tree/master/logo) or [poster](https://github.com/Trustroots/media/tree/master/poster) and check instructions how to print your own t-shirts:

- [WikiHow](http://www.wikihow.com/Print-on-T%E2%80%90Shirts)
- [Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/howto/t-shirt+print/)
- [Laura Kampf´s emergency screen printing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MmobqpBUfQo)
