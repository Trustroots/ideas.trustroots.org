Hey folks!

Near one of the longest virgin beaches of Spain, the mixture of work, sun and sea brought this great energy into the community of Trustroots: lots of new people ready to team up and work online! Growing the roots!

## Tech boost

![](/images/2018/10/Screenshot-2018-10-24-at-00.04.04.png)

It's really nice to see this much geekery from 10 different people in just one week

Online activity on Slack and GitHub has increased massively. A large part of this flurry of activity is thanks to [Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.digitalocean.com/) where we show up on the main page at times.

## What's next

Guaka picked up [Akronix](https://github.com/akronix) on the way, which led to some pair programming. Akronix has already left but will present Trustroots at the [Hackmeeting in Malaga](https://twitter.com/simbiosisfest/status/1053294678169272320) from 1st to 4th November.

Tech wise, the top priority this year is to add reference-type functionality to contacts between profiles. You can follow some of that [on GitHub](https://github.com/Trustroots/trustroots/labels/trust).

## Spreading the word

Together with Lisanna we have been working on the interface and user experience. We also revived [our instagram](https://www.instagram.com/trustroots_org/), joining other travellers on the globe. We want to get merchandise to travellers with blogs and social media following, through [our shop](https://shop.trustroots.org/).

## Goals

These are the goals we set ourselves:

### Reach out to (new) members

We gotta do more outreach. Got a travel blog, write about Trustroots! We also welcome guest posts on our own blog, if you like writing about anything related to Trustroots, travel, trust if you want to tell the world about your experience with Trustroots.

### Wakey wakey support team

Helena started taking care of support queries. Let us know if you want to help with this.

### Safety team

Wherever your journey brings you to, remain trustworthy yet aware. We're so many on the go, to keep Trustroots safe and trusty we're adding safety features. We are here for you, building a community.  Wanna help?

## More seriously

On a serious note: we are very aware of some issues around hospitality exchange networks in the past and there's a lot of that **we don't want**:

- turn into a for profit corporation
- raise 20M$ VC money (and waste it all)
- a board or an executive team that's detached from the community
- dictators and censorship (really, this happened)
- endless discussions

And this is what **we *do* want**:

- [free software forever](https://github.com/trustroots/)
- [transparency around finance](https://www.trustroots.org/foundation)
- move forward together, with board, volunteers, hackers, community
- [lots](https://www.trustroots.org/statistics) of awesome people on our network
- actions (speak louder than words)
