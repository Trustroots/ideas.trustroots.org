Let's see if this becomes a habit, but the idea is to highlight all the small improvements and continuous efforts that go on Trustroots, thanks to [all our volunteers](https://www.trustroots.org/team)! 👏

---

[**The search**](https://www.trustroots.org/search) got a bigger upgrade under the hood — it's now smoother and faster, especially on mobile. Visuals got refreshed a bit, but primarily this change will also unlock any future improvements to Search. Stay tuned!

Already visited dots are now a bit transparent, and currently selected offer is black.

## Other

- New [Circle](https://www.trustroots.org/circles) descriptions and images for quite a few circles.
- New areas of Trustroots available for [translation](https://team.trustroots.org/Translating-Getting-Started.html): contact page, statistics page, and search map. This will get us closer to releasing translated Trustroots to everyone.
- Fix our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/trustroots) & [Twitter](https://twitter.com/trustroots) profile links at the site, and both of those profiles have been much more active recently, too.
- Fixed profile description jumping while editing it.
- Added API for blocking users (half the job done, now the interface...)
- New experiences feature had boatloads of progress, bringing it closer to public release.
- We sent out [a newsletter](https://trustrootsideas.wordpress.com/2020/12/30/trustroots-travel-stories-and-latest-news/)! 🎉
- We kept helping you out by the support team. ❤️
