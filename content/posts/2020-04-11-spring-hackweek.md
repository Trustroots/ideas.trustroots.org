With a pandemic rolling over Europe and borders being closed, it might not be the time for traveling right now. But we think it's a good time for working on a traveler's network and pushing it to the next level.

So, we didn't cancel our hackweek that we were looking forward to. Instead, we're doing it [online](https://wiki.trustroots.org/en/Hackweek_Spring_2020). While a lot of people are currently struggling with the challenges of remote collaboration, we're already used to it.

![](/images/2020/04/2020-04-11-hackweek.png)

Today, we started the hackweek and we're happy some new faces joined the gang! There are a lot of different ways anyone can contribute to Trustroots. If you want to be a part of that, [get in touch](https://trustroots.org/support)! You don't have to be a hacker to join the hackweek ;)

That all being said, we all value in-person contact very much and we're looking forward to have a hackweek in the physical world once that is possible again.
