mrkvon and banan arrived here a while ago and have been excellent at getting me deeper into the code base. This led to some  cosmetic changes on the site, including edit buttons on some static pages such as the [guide](https://www.trustroots.org/guide).

Related to that, we now have a wiki: [wiki.trustroots.org](https://wiki.trustrooots.org/) - it's actually not new, we simply hijacked Couchwiki, which is a wiki I was running since 2009 (when couchsurfing decided to take down the CouchSurfing Wiki - it took a couple more years to pull the final trigger).

And then we have a nicer [developers.trustroots.org](https://developers.trustroots.org) that can help with getting more people active.

akronix returned for a couple more days, and progressed with a text-based user search function. This will go live really soon. mrkvon and banan have done great work on

- [moving to React](https://developers.trustroots.org/React.html)
- moving forward some of the older [pull requests](https://github.com/Trustroots/trustroots/pulls)
- adding translations (i18n) to the site - [wanna help translate?](https://meta.trustroots.org/t/lets-translate-trustroots/59/3)
- work on implementing references - it would help a lot if you sign up for a test account at [dev.trustroots.org](https://dev.trustroots.org/) and play with this

We now have a Discourse forum: [meta.trustroots.org](https://meta.trustroots.org/) is a place to coordinate and discuss ideas and more.

And then I'm really happy *[triphopping](http://triphopping.com/)* Kenny (check his [awesome podcast](http://www.freestyletravelshow.com/)!) arrived here yesterday, he has some great insights how we can make Trustroots more awesome and grow Trustroots in the US.
