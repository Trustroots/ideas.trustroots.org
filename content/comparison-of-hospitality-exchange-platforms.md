Here is a factual comparison table of some hospitality exchange projects. This is by no means a complete list, but it's covering the ones that are often considered an alternative to Couchsurfing.

Dots reflect our [values](https://www.trustroots.org/about): 🟢 available or aligned; 🔴 unavailable or not aligned; 🟡 partial, preview or planned; ⚪ not verified. Text labels give the details. For advertising, **No** is green.

- **CS** — Couchsurfing — *"Share your life"*
- **BW** — [BeWelcome](https://www.bewelcome.org/) — *"Open your door to the world"*
- **WS** — [Warmshowers](https://www.warmshowers.org/) — *"A community for touring cyclists and hosts"*
- **TR** — [Trustroots](https://www.trustroots.org/) — *"Travellers' community for sharing, hosting and getting people together"*
- **CO** — [Couchers.org](https://couchers.org/) — *"Free forever. Community-led. Non-profit."*

## Federation and decentralisation

Here, technical federation or decentralisation means enabling identities, data or interactions to work across independently operated services. A federation of organisations is a different kind of decentralisation.

| Platform | Federation / decentralisation efforts |
| --- | --- |
| Trustroots / Nostroots | 🟡 [Active Nostr-based development](https://github.com/Trustroots/nostroots): an app and relay-based infrastructure, with a goal of portable identities and connections across compatible projects. Full migration of Trustroots is still work in progress. |
| BeWelcome | ⚪ Current technical federation efforts not verified in this review. [Open-source software](https://github.com/BeWelcome/rox) allows independent development and forks; that alone does not connect separate installations. |
| Couchers.org | ⚪ Current technical federation efforts not verified in this review. Its [open-source code](https://github.com/Couchers-org/couchers) can be reused, but this is not evidence of shared identities or data between installations. |
| Warmshowers | ⚪ Current technical federation or decentralisation efforts not verified. |
| Couchsurfing | ⚪ Current technical federation or decentralisation efforts not verified. |
| Servas | 🟢 [Federation of national and multinational groups](https://servas.org/terms-use), with local membership processes. This describes organisational structure; technical federation of ServasOnline was not verified. |
| WWOOF | 🟢 [Decentralised non-profit network of national organisations](https://help.wwoof.net/hc/en-us/articles/16654242218525-How-is-WWOOF-different-from-other-networks). This describes organisational structure; [memberships are generally separate by country](https://help.wwoof.net/hc/en-us/articles/16654242038941-What-does-a-WWOOFer-membership-cost). |
| Workaway, HelpX and Worldpackers | ⚪ Current technical federation or decentralisation efforts not verified. |

This is a review of published information as of **13 October 2026**, not a claim that unverified projects have no such plans.

## Volunteering and learning exchanges

These platforms connect travellers with hosts for agreed help or practical learning, often including accommodation. WWOOF describes its model as an educational and cultural exchange focused on organic farming.

| Characteristic | [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/) | [WWOOF](https://wwoof.net/) | [HelpX](https://www.helpx.net/) | [Worldpackers](https://www.worldpackers.com/) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | [Families, community projects, farms, NGOs, house/pet sitting and other exchanges](https://www.workaway.info/en/info/how-it-works/workawayer) | [Organic farming, sustainable living and hands-on learning through a non-profit network](https://wwoof.net/) | [Farms, homestays, hostels and other practical-help exchanges](https://www.helpx.net/) | [Hostels, social projects, NGOs, ecovillages and communities](https://www.worldpackers.com/how_it_works) |
| Price for travellers | 🔴 [From €59/year solo or €69/year for a pair](https://www.workaway.info/en/signup/workawayer); premium plans cost more | 🟡 [Varies by destination](https://help.wwoof.net/hc/en-us/articles/16654242038941-What-does-a-WWOOFer-membership-cost): Portugal €20/year solo or €30 dual; France €28/€34; UK £25/£35 | 🟡 [€20 for two years, single or twin Premier membership](https://www.helpx.net/registerhelper); free profiles have limited contact access | 🔴 [From US$59/year solo or US$69/year for a pair](https://www.worldpackers.com/get_verified); premium plans cost more |
| Membership coverage | 🟢 [One membership covers hosts worldwide](https://www.workaway.info/en/info/how-it-works/workawayer) | 🟡 [Membership is generally per country](https://help.wwoof.net/hc/en-us/articles/16654242038941-What-does-a-WWOOFer-membership-cost); WWOOF Independents covers countries without a national organisation | 🟢 [Premier membership works worldwide](https://www.helpx.net/registerhelper) | 🟢 [Annual plans cover worldwide opportunities](https://www.worldpackers.com/get_verified) |
| Typical contribution | [Around 4–5 hours/day, five days/week](https://www.workaway.info/en/info/how-it-works/workawayer), agreed with the host | [Around a half-day, typically 4–5 hours, with days off](https://wwoof.net/news/a-first-timers-guide-to-wwoofing-everything-you-need-to-know/); agree details with the host | [Around four hours/day](https://helpx.net/registerchoice); arrangements vary | [Often 20–25 hours/week for operational tasks or 15–20 for creative tasks](https://help.worldpackers.com/en/hc/how-many-hours-of-help-should-i-ask-for); check each listing |
| Accommodation | 🟢 [Normally included in the agreed exchange](https://www.workaway.info/en/info/how-it-works/workawayer) | 🟢 [Included](https://wwoof.net/) | 🟢 [Normally included](https://helpx.net/registerchoice) | 🟢 [Included in the exchange](https://www.worldpackers.com/how_it_works) |
| Meals | 🟢 [Normally included; confirm the arrangement](https://www.workaway.info/en/info/how-it-works/workawayer) | 🟢 [Included](https://wwoof.net/) | 🟢 [Normally included; confirm the arrangement](https://helpx.net/registerchoice) | 🟡 [Depends on the listing](https://www.worldpackers.com/how_it_works) |
| Other host charges | Check the listing and agreed arrangement | 🟢 [No money exchanged between host and WWOOFer for the visit](https://wwoof.net/) | Check the listing and agreed arrangement | 🟡 [Some projects charge additional fees](https://www.worldpackers.com/positions/96403), separately from membership |

Prices and published arrangements checked **13 October 2026** using the linked official sources. Workaway prices above are the euro prices displayed when checked; Worldpackers prices are in US dollars. Agree the activities, hours, days off, accommodation, meals and any extra charges before accepting a placement.

## Why Trustroots is exploring Nostr

We want members to retain their identity and connections even if a particular app, server or organising team changes. Nostr offers a shared protocol with key-based identities and independently operated relays, giving different applications a foundation for working together.

Through [Nostroots](https://github.com/Trustroots/nostroots), our goal is to make hospitality and other sharing projects less dependent on a single operator, and let communities build compatible tools. This is ongoing development; the existing Trustroots service has not been fully migrated.

Read [How we keep Trustroots free forever and ever and ever]({{< relref "posts/2025-11-08-keep-trustroots-free-forever-ever.md" >}}) for the community's reasons for this direction.
