> **Historical comparison:** last updated 24 May 2020. Figures, features and links reflect that period and may have changed.

Here is a factual comparison table of some hospitality exchange projects. This is by no means a complete list, but it's covering the ones that are often considered an alternative to Couchsurfing.

Colors are biased towards our [values](https://www.trustroots.org/about) (e.g. advertising or corporation model are in red).

- **CS** — [Couchsurfing](https://www.couchsurfing.com/) — *"Share your life"*
- **HC** — [HospitalityClub](http://www.hospitalityclub.org/) — *"Bringing people together"*
- **BW** — [BeWelcome](https://www.bewelcome.org/) — *"Open your door to the world"*
- **WS** — [Warmshowers](https://www.warmshowers.org/) — *"A community for touring cyclists and hosts"*
- **TR** — [Trustroots](https://www.trustroots.org/) — *"Travellers' community for sharing, hosting and getting people together"*

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Legal status | US [C Corporation](https://blog.couchsurfing.com/a-new-era-for-couchsurfing/) | No legal status, sole proprietor | French [non-profit association](http://www.bewelcome.org/wiki/BeVolunteer) | US [Non-profit](https://www.warmshowers.org/faq#t24n3806) 501(c)(3) | UK [Non-profit Foundation](https://www.trustroots.org/foundation) |
| Community | Very active | Not active | Active | Active | Active |
| Members (active and non-active) | 15M ([10/2016](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ROufiiyQjr8&t=11m24s)) | 792K ([09/2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitalityclub)) | 104K ([9/2017](http://www.bewelcome.org/stats)) | 127K ([5/2017](https://www.warmshowers.org/country_count)) | 44K ([5/2020](https://www.trustroots.org/statistics)) |

## More about our values
